About us

This app is inspired by https://quiz.thetasoft.com/ and built as an educational project by two trainees from the Dutch Tax Service, together with two experienced Dutch umpires who work for the Dutch Baseball Association KNBSB.

The questions are based on the most recent OBR rules, generic for every country in the world.

This is the first version of the app. We have many ideas for new features, like

  • Choose language
  • Suggest question
  • Keep personal progress

More ideas are much appreciated. Please contact us. Have fun!
